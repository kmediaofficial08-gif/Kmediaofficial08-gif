# K Media | AI Integration and Operations Consulting

I help operators and small businesses stop losing time and jobs to missed calls, slow follow-up, and work that only lives in one person's head.

My focus is simple: calls answered, leads followed up, less routine labor, tighter operations, and better visibility and control.

## Who this is for

- Owner-operators and small teams who miss calls they cannot afford to miss
- Businesses where leads go cold because follow-up is slow or inconsistent
- Teams where key processes depend on one person remembering how it all works

## What I do

- **Workflow audit:** a live look at how your business actually runs, and where time and revenue leak out
- **AI blueprint:** a clear, custom plan for what to fix first and how the pieces fit together
- **Implementation:** the systems built, connected, and tested so they work in your day-to-day operations
- **AI phone receptionist and follow-up systems:** every call answered and every lead followed up, around the clock

## How it works

One repeatable process, nine steps:

**Discover → Audit → Design → Automate → Integrate → Test → Deploy → Optimize → Manage**
